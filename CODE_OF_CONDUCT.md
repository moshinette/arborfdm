# Code of conduct

ArborFDM follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be welcoming and patient: many people here are new to 3D printing, to Python, or to GitHub, and write in their second language (English and French are both fine).
- Criticise code, settings and prints, not people. Failed prints are useful data, not a reason to mock anyone.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexualised content, or publishing someone's private information.
- Respect creators: do not share or ask for pirated or paid models, and do not post other people's work without permission.
- No spam or advertising unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and temporarily or permanently block people who do not follow these rules, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer listed in `README.md` (section "Contact"). Reports are handled confidentially.
